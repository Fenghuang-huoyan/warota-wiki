Hats are the class system. The store is explicit about the rule and sparing about the roster. This page copies the rule and the examples, and it stops.

## The rule

The feature heading is "Pick Your Hat, Change Your Job Class."

> In this world, hats carry the goddess's power. The hat you wear determines your Job Class.

The next sentences sort the examples into two bands and then give the count:

- Gathering and crafting professions, "like Farmers and Lumberjacks."
- Combat roles, "like Warriors and Mages."
- "Choose from more than 20 Job Classes."
- You can experiment, swap hats to match the situation, and look for a playstyle.

"Like" matters. Farmer, Lumberjack, Warrior, and Mage are examples inside a claim of more than 20. They are the entire player-facing name list in the English about text. The official site's English SPEC block does not add another name.

![Characters in wide hats face a stacked green creature in a desert of cacti and broken columns](/images/official/steam/ss-03.jpg)

Store art shows characters in wide straw hats on the farm and in other brims during fights. The inventory screenshot on this page's banner includes hat-shaped icons in the grid. Those pictures support "you wear a hat." They do not label a class on the icon.

## Villager hats

A second block, "Team Up with Your Villagers and Assign Them Jobs," uses the same object for a different person. Assigning a hat to a villager is how they get a job.

The sentence names three:

| Example in the store sentence | What that sentence says they do |
| --- | --- |
| Farmer | Tilling and harvesting the fields |
| Chef | Keeping everyone fed |
| Guard | Defending the village from invading monsters |

Farmer appears in both the player examples and the villager sentence. Chef and Guard appear in the villager sentence and are not repeated in the "more than 20 Job Classes" sentence. This page does not decide whether Chef and Guard are two of the 20, or village-only roles. Steam did not say.

The same paragraph says villagers you bring along "each with their own unique traits and strengths." No trait list follows. The party screenshot shows several portraits with different hats beside a field. The names in that UI are Japanese. They are not added here as an English class list.

How the village uses those jobs day to day is on [Village](/village). The Guard line is the one defensive assignment the store connects to monster attacks, which is the subject of [Demon King](/demon-king).

## Blessings are not hats

Attack buffs, recovery, and elemental attributes come from prayers at Goddess Statues. That system is written up under [Blessings](/guide#blessings-from-goddess-statues). The store presents it as a stack you build on top of play, and it presents the hat as the thing that sets the Job Class. Nothing on the page says a blessing replaces a hat, or that a hat is a blessing.

<Callout kind="note" title="What a class guide would need">
A complete hat list, the stat each class changes, how a hat is crafted or found, and whether swapping is limited in combat. None of those were on the Steam page, the demo listing, or the official site on 12 November 2026. Early Access is still planned for 13 November 2026. When the client prints a roster, the client is the list.
</Callout>
