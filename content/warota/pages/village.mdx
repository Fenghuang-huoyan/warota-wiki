### Expanding Upward: The Carpenter's Trial

Village building space in Luna is limited by terrain and monster paths. A key progression feature in Early Access is vertical stacking:

1. Early on, each villager requires a separate ground-level house.
2. Completing **Carpenter's Trial 1 (大工の試練・1)** unlocks multi-story construction.
3. Once unlocked, players can stack houses up to **4 stories high**, significantly reducing the footprint required for population expansion.

## Villagers: Unlocking, Housing, and Job Assignment

Villagers are not just decorative NPCs; they form the automated workforce and defense militia of your settlement.

### How Villagers Arrive
- **Housing condition**: Villagers arrive as you build new houses and expand community facilities. If no empty home is available, new residents will not settle.
- **Roster expansion**: Constructing specialized facilities (such as the Farm Shed and workshops) attracts villagers suitable for those trades.

### Job Assignment via Hats
Villagers have no inherent locked class until you give them a hat. Equipping a villager with a hat determines their daily routine:

### Shared Storage Logistics
Villager automation relies on **Storage Boxes (収納箱)**:
- Craft and place storage boxes centrally near fields and workshops.
- Villagers automatically fetch tools and ingredients from storage boxes and deposit completed harvests and logs.
- Villagers take cooked food directly from storage boxes, eliminating the need to micromanage individual inventories.

## Preparing Village Defenses for Demon King Live

Building in Luna must always account for your neighbor: the Idol Demon King Platina.

- **The 5-Day Cycle**: Demon King Live concerts trigger a massive monster stampede every **5 in-game days**.
- **Funneling with Fences**: Place wooden and iron fencing (柵・鉄の柵) around farms and living quarters to funnel invading monsters into choke points.
- **Guard Deployment**: Assign villagers with Guard or combat hats to hold defensive choke points alongside the player.
- **Post-Platina Building Freedom**: Defeating Demon King Platina at the climax of the Platina Chapter halts the recurring frenzy stampedes, allowing players to build and expand freely without 5-day raid interruptions.
