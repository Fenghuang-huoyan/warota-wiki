Following the game's Early Access launch on **13 November 2026**, the village system is fully playable through the **Platina Chapter**. You arrive in the fantasy world of Luna as a Bounder, tasked with restoring a dilapidated home right next door to the Demon King's castle.

This guide details the structures, agricultural plots, villager mechanics, and automation loops available in the Early Access release. Where exact ingredient amounts, specific facility blueprints, or late-game systems are not documented in official public sources, they are explicitly marked as **Unverified / 未検証**.

### Expanding Upward: The Carpenter's Trial (Unverified / 未検証)

Village building space in Luna is limited by terrain and monster paths. A key progression feature reported in Early Access gameplay is vertical stacking:

1. Early on, each villager requires a separate ground-level house.
2. Completing **Carpenter's Trial 1 (大工の試練・1)** unlocks multi-story construction.
3. Once unlocked, players can stack houses up to **4 stories high**, significantly reducing the footprint required for population expansion.

*(Note: While vertical buildings appear in store screenshots, the Carpenter's Trial quest and exact 4-story limit are unverified in official store descriptions).*

## Villagers: Unlocking, Housing, and Job Assignment

Villagers form the automated workforce and defense militia of your settlement.

### How Villagers Arrive
- **Housing condition**: Villagers arrive as you build new houses and expand community facilities (Verified on Steam: more buildings bring more villagers).
- **Roster expansion**: Constructing specialized facilities attracts villagers suitable for those trades (Unverified / 未検証 in-game report).

## Preparing Village Defenses for Demon King Live

Building in Luna must always account for your neighbor: the Idol Demon King Platina.

- **Concert Raids**: When Demon King Platina holds a concert, a massive wave of hyped-up monsters stampedes straight toward your village (Verified on Steam). In early gameplay, these raids are reported to occur roughly every 5 in-game days (Unverified / 未検証).
- **Funneling with Fences**: Wooden fences appear in official store screenshots; using wooden and iron fencing (柵・鉄の柵) to funnel invading monsters into choke points is a player-reported strategy (Unverified / 未検証).
- **Guard Deployment**: Assign villagers with Guard or combat hats to hold defensive choke points alongside the player (Verified on Steam: Guards defend against invading monsters).
- **Post-Platina Building Freedom**: Defeating Demon King Platina at the climax of the Platina Chapter is reported to halt recurring frenzy stampedes, allowing players to build and expand freely (Unverified / 未検証).
