A tip page for a concert defense, written before Early Access unlocks, can only repeat the instructions the store already gives. Those instructions are short. They are also the whole official plan this site is willing to print.

## What the concert is

The story block introduces her as the Demon King, a pop idol the monsters adore. The announcement that starts Demon King Live is quoted on the store:

> This world belongs to us! ♡ Listen up, my lovely monsters! Go wild, break whatever you want, and have some fun! ♡

The feature block shortens the setup. "Your next-door neighbor? Yeah... it's the Demon King's castle." Every time the Idol Demon King holds a concert, hordes of frenzied monsters descend on the village. The short description calls that horde "a massive wave of hyped-up monsters."

The English about text does not give the idol a personal name. It also does not say how often "every time" is on a clock. A night, a chapter, and a timer are three different claims, and only the concert itself is stated.

![A blonde performer stands on a concert stage flanked by castle turrets, speakers, and spotlights](/images/official/steam/ss-12.jpg)

The stage screenshot matches the idol-concert pitch: a blonde performer, speakers, spotlights, and castle-shaped scenery. It is a key art frame from the store. It is not a fight timeline.

## The tips that are actually on the page

The store's own "how you deal with it" paragraph is four actions:

1. **Team up with your villagers.** The villager section says a hat assigns their job, and that you can bring them into a fight. The defensive example is the **Guard**, who defends the village from invading monsters. Other named jobs (Farmer, Chef, Lumberjack, Warrior, Mage) are on [Hats](/hats). The store does not say the Guard is required, or that other hats cannot fight.
2. **Fortify your defenses.** This sits next to the village pitch of more than 30 buildings. The page never says which building is a wall, a trap, or a barricade. [Village](/village) keeps that limit.
3. **Hold back the waves.** "Waves" is the store's word for the monsters threatening the home. No count, no gap between waves, and no fail condition is printed.
4. **Defeat the powerful bosses.** The store says that after those bosses, "the hidden truth of this world will slowly come to light." It does not name a boss in the English prose.

![A night village fight with a large green monster, pink buildings, fireworks, and a hotbar](/images/official/steam/ss-01.jpg)

The night-fight screenshot shows a large green monster in a village, fireworks, and smaller characters, with Japanese objective text on the right. Another frame shows a campfire and a tower at night, and another shows a cave fight with a purple area effect. They confirm the store is advertising combat in and around the village. They are not a boss order, and this page does not translate the UI into one.

Blessings are the other lever the store offers for a fight: pray at Goddess Statues, then stack attack, recovery, and elemental buffs. The examples and the missing table are in the [guide](/guide#blessings-from-goddess-statues).

## Where Early Access says combat stands

The Early Access notes say combat, with village building, crafting, and exploration, is playable through the **Platina Chapter**. They also say the Early Access period is for balancing, controls, polish, and new content, and that they expect it to last about a year.

That is a scope statement. It is not a walkthrough of the chapter, and it is not a schedule of concerts inside the chapter. On 12 November 2026 the full game was still `coming_soon: true`, with the planned date **13 Nov 2026**. The defense you can rehearse from public text, today, is the four-step paragraph above.
